RESPONSIVE BEHAVIOUR
- Screens wider than 767px use hero.mp4.
- Screens 767px wide or narrower use hero-mobile.mp4.
- Both versions autoplay muted, loop, and play inline.
- The page uses object-fit: cover to fill the hero area, with the existing
  /hero_background.webp image as a poster/fallback while video loads or when
  reduced-motion preferences are enabled.
- If the mobile video cannot load, the page falls back to the landscape version.
